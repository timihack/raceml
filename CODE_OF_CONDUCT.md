# RaceML Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RaceML welcomes people who want to improve machine learning software, regardless of experience, background, or identity. Participation in project spaces requires respectful, professional conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical ideas on their merits and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Give constructive feedback on code, tests, benchmarks, and documentation.
- Respect contributors' time and experience; welcome questions asked in good faith.
- Credit other people's work and respect licences and attribution requirements.
- Keep private reports and personal information confidential.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive conduct, repeated bad-faith arguments, or retaliation for reporting misconduct.

## Scope

This policy applies to RaceML repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and project-organized community spaces. It also applies when someone represents RaceML publicly.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately by emailing **timihack.it@gmail.com** with the subject **RaceML Code of Conduct Report**. Include what happened, where and when it happened, and any relevant links or screenshots you are comfortable sharing. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer will acknowledge reports when possible, review the information confidentially, and communicate any action that can appropriately be shared. If the report concerns the person receiving this email, an independent review cannot be guaranteed until another trusted maintainer is appointed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly, limit access to reports, seek context where appropriate, and take proportionate action. Responses may include a warning, comment removal, temporary participation limits, or a ban. A maintainer named in a report must not decide the outcome of that report. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

## Appeals

A person subject to enforcement may request review by a different maintainer where one is available. Decisions and sanctions may be revisited when new evidence emerges.

This is a RaceML-specific community policy. It is not presented as an unmodified copy of the Contributor Covenant.
